# Lumen Notes 1.0 launch brief

Lumen Notes is a local-first notes app for small research teams.

- Launch date: 2026-11-18
- Audience: research leads at 10–50 person teams
- Goal: 2,000 paid seats in the first 60 days
- Channels: product newsletter, two partner webinars, a launch post
